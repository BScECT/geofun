# Advanced least squares

In the previous chapter you learned about linear regression, which can be used to quantify relationships between physical parameters, and to predict outcomes of a certain process / property (dependent variables) based on observations of another variable (independent variable). In this chapter, we will give some (other) examples of linear models where least squares estimation can be applied, and will specifically look into problems where the precision of the different observations is not constant.

Contents:
* [Estimation problems](2:estimationprob): introduction to different types of problems and linear models
* [Exercise notebook: fitting different models](2:nb_fitting): apply least squares with different linear models applied to the same data
* [Heteroscedasticity and weighted least squares](2:wls): dealing with different variances and how to deal with that
* [Exercise notebook: changing the weights](2:wls_nb): check your understanding of weighted least squares
* [Notebook: sampling](2:sampling_nb): demo many realizations of observations, their average
* [Exercise notebook: factors influencing precision](2:confint_nb): see how shape of confidence interval changes depending on different factors
