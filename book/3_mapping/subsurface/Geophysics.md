# Investigating subsurface properties with Geophysics

# DC-resistivity
- Various names: direct-current resistivity (DCR) method, geoelectric method, electrical resistivity tomography (ERT). 
- Current (I) flow in subsurface excited by injection at very low frequency. 
- Solely electric field (as voltage U) measured.
- Rock-units can be distinguished via their resistivities. Filling of pore spaces in the rocks influences the resistivity.
- Apparent resistivities ρ = (K*ΔV)/I depend on geometry factor K and plotted as pseudo-section. Our data!
- Different arrays offer different resolving power for different structures.
- Maximum depth of penetration very roughly 1/4 of largest electrode separation.
- Geophysical inversion algorithms are required to obtain a resistivity model (properties) from the measured data.
